# Brief: cram pack (tunable)

Night before and morning of. **No new material**: everything comes from existing packs, the deck, formula sheet,
corrections log, `progress/errors.md` and the past papers.
1. Page 1: must-remember formulas only (`tmonepage`, must fit one page).
2. Page 2: one-liners ordered by (a) how often their topic is asked, (b) the student's wrong/hesitant deck items.
3. Top 10 repeated mistakes from `progress/errors.md` (if fewer, take traps from packs).
4. Last year's paper solved in exam style (reuse pack solutions; verify again).
5. Exam-day checklist: units on every answer, what is printed on the paper, book policy, calculator mode
   (degrees/radians), standard values to state as assumptions, time per mark.
Output `cram/cram.(pdf|md)`. Full verify pass.
